IMPORTANT BEFORE SHOWING OR LAUNCHING FOR A REAL CLIENT
1. Replace Willow & Grace Care with the client's actual business name and logo.
2. Replace the placeholder phone, email, town, opening hours and all service information.
3. Replace demo images with images the client owns or has permission to use.
4. Verify all services, staff qualifications, registration and regulator information. Do not claim CQC or other registration unless confirmed.
5. Replace sample vacancies and article copy with approved real content.
6. The contact form is a front-end demonstration only. It does NOT send emails or store submissions. Connect it to a secure form handler or the client's approved system and test it before launch.
7. Replace the privacy notice with a complete policy written for the actual business. Review cookie/analytics requirements before enabling tracking.
8. Add the client's real Google Maps location or service-area map.
9. Test all pages, links, forms, accessibility and mobile layouts before publication.
