Understanding and reassurance

Support that honours each person's story.

Every person is different. Familiar routines, patient communication and respect for individual preferences can make a meaningful difference.

A person-first approach

See the person, not just the diagnosis.

Support should recognise each person's identity, relationships, interests and abilities — and involve them in decisions as much as possible.

  • Use calm, respectful and clear communication
  • Learn about familiar routines and meaningful activities
  • Work with families and agreed representatives
  • Promote comfort, dignity and choice

Any dementia-related support offered by a real provider must reflect staff competence, assessed needs and the provider's authorised service scope.

Talk with us about your situation.

We can listen to what support you are exploring and explain whether the provider's services may be suitable. This is not a substitute for medical advice or an urgent care service.
