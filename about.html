Our story

People are never just a care plan.

Willow & Grace Care is a demonstration care provider created to show how a local agency could introduce its values, people and services online.

Our approach is built on listening carefully, understanding individual preferences and involving people in decisions about their support. We believe the most helpful care protects dignity, encourages independence and makes room for the things that bring joy.

  • We listen before we make recommendations
  • We respect each person's home, culture and choices
  • We communicate clearly with people and families
